# Unfplap

Do your mares.

## how

Copy `.pck` from releases into your mod folder.

Requires Home: Equestria `0.1.1-maredev`.

Desktop only for now

## Controls

| Input       | Action                                                            |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Y`         | Start an available encounter                                      |
| Mouse ↕     | Thrust during the act                                               |
| `W` / `S`   | Lean the act camera forward / back (experimental)                 |
| `Q` / `E`   | Lean diagonally; also cue a kiss or shoulder reach (experimental) |
| `A` / `D`   | Caress her left / right flank in horse pose, including afterglow (experimental) |
| `F` (hold)  | Kiss while fully shoulder-leaning in horse pose (experimental)    |
| `H`         | Toggle automatic lean return; hold is default, latches A/D caress and lets S recentre roll (experimental) |
| Mouse right | Smack her flank during the act (experimental)                     |
| `T`         | Talk to her; a talk cue can make her ask for your voice            |
| Hold `Y`    | Afterglow after her first climax / stop early / end afterglow     |

## Features

- **Relationship-gated encounters** — Adult mares and stallions only, with consent and refusals
  for both player forms.
- **Two poses** — Supports missionary on a bed or couch and standing horse.
- **Proactive mares** — A ready mare may ask for sex herself; a private standing mare presents for horse, and `Y` still accepts.
- **Mouse thrust readiness** — Build her arousal with real thrusts; she can climax repeatedly while you keep thrusting.
- **Per-mare taste** — Each mare has intensity, rhythm, touch, talk, warmth and recovery.
- **Thrust and taste editors** — Tune mouse sensitivity and each pony's taste in `Settings → Unfplap`.
- **Reactive dialogue** — Her cues react to mood, rhythm, touch and what you say to her.
- **HUD** — Shows the act controls list, readiness meter, optional mood cues and climax count.
- **Act camera** — Offers a controlled lean camera with optional thrust bob and motion.
- **Act actions** — Leaning cues kisses and shoulder reaches; A/D caress a flank, F kisses at a full shoulder lean, and right-click smacks one.
- **Cuddle override** — Lets an encounter begin while a cuddle is active (experimental).
- **Presentation** — Adds blush, eyes, ears, tail, jiggle, audio and wing motion.
- **Aftermath** — Ends with a held afterglow for either pose; in horse afterglow, staying close and caressing earns a small extra reward, then short aftercare.
- **Memory and witnesses** — Intimacy is remembered, and nearby awake adults can witness it.
